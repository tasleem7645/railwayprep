### 1. 🚆 RRB ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot) — Official CEN 01/2026 (11,127 Posts)
```
[Stage 1: CBT-1 Screening] ──► [Stage 2: CBT-2 (Part A + B)] ──► [Stage 3: CBAT (Psycho)] ──► [Stage 4: DV (50:50 Merit)] ──► [Stage 5: Medical (A-1)] ──► [Final Panel]
```

#### 📅 Important Dates (CEN 01/2026):
* **Date of Indicative Notice:** 21.03.2026
* **Opening Date of Online Application:** 15.05.2026 (00:00 Hrs)
* **Closing Date of Online Application:** 14.06.2026 (23:59 Hrs)
* **Last Date for Application Fee Payment:** 16.06.2026 (23:59 Hrs)
* **Modification Window for Corrections:** 17.06.2026 to 26.06.2026 (Fee: ₹250)

#### 💰 Pay Scale & Age Limit:
* **Pay Level:** Level-2 in 7th CPC (Initial Pay ₹19,900/- per month + DA, HRA, Running Allowance).
* **Age Limit (as on 01-07-2026):** 18 – 30 Years (OBC-NCL: +3 Years relaxation, SC/ST: +5 Years relaxation).

#### 🎓 Prescribed Minimum Educational Qualification:
1. **Matriculation / SSLC + ITI** from recognized NCVT/SCVT institutions in: Fitter, Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Millwright/Maintenance Mechanic, Mechanic Radio & TV, Electronics Mechanic, Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Wireman, Tractor Mechanic, Armature & Coil Winder, Mechanic Diesel, Heat Engine, Turner, Machinist, RAC Mechanic.
2. **OR Matriculation / SSLC + Course Completed Act Apprenticeship (CCAA)** in the trades mentioned above.
3. **OR Matriculation / SSLC + 3-year Diploma** in Mechanical / Electrical / Electronics / Automobile Engineering (or combination streams).
4. **OR Engineering Degree (B.E./B.Tech)** in the specified disciplines in lieu of Diploma.
*(Note: PwBD candidates are Not Suitable for ALP).*

#### 👁️ Strict A-1 Medical Standards & Critical New 2026 Rules:
* **Distant Vision:** 6/6, 6/6 without glasses with fogging test (must not accept +2D).
* **Near Vision:** Sn: 0.6, 0.6 without glasses.
* **Color Vision Tests:** Ishihara Colour Vision test, Binocular Vision, Field of Vision, Night Vision, Mesopic Vision mandatory.
* **Prohibited Surgery:** LASIK or any other surgical procedure to correct refractive error is **strictly disqualified** (Proforma declaration Annexure-VI mandatory).
* **🚨 MANDATORY NEW 2026 CLAUSE (Self-Screening Medical Declaration):**
  * Candidates must obtain signed **Annexure VIII-A (Eye Specialist)** and **Annexure VIII-B (General Physician)** from a **Central/State Government Hospital or Medical College**.
  * Original reports with doctor's seal **must be submitted on CBAT exam day**. Failure to produce this will lead to immediate debarment from CBAT!

#### 📝 Selection Stages & Exam Pattern:
1. **Stage 1: CBT-1 (Screening Test):**
   * **Pattern:** 75 Questions | 60 Minutes | 75 Marks | Negative marking: 1/3rd (-0.33).
   * **Subject Breakdown:**
     * Mental Ability / Reasoning: 25 Questions (25 Marks)
     * Mathematics: 20 Questions (20 Marks)
     * General Science (10th CBSE Physics, Chemistry, Life Sciences): 20 Questions (20 Marks)
     * General Awareness & Current Affairs: 10 Questions (10 Marks)
   * **Qualifying Pass %:** UR & EWS 40%, OBC(NCL) 30%, SC 30%, ST 25%.
   * **Shortlisting for CBT-2:** Exactly **15 times** the number of RRB-wise vacancies. Marks of CBT-1 are NOT added to final merit panel.

2. **Stage 2: CBT-2 (Two Parts in single sitting - Total Duration 2 Hours 30 Minutes, 175 Questions):**
   * **Part A (Merit Decider - 100 Questions / 90 Minutes / 100 Marks):**
     * **Negative Marking:** 1/3rd (-0.33).
     * **Cutoff:** UR/EWS 40%, OBC 30%, SC 30%, ST 25%.
     * **Subjects:**
       * **Basic Science & Engineering (BSED - 40 Qs):** Engineering Drawing (Projections, Views, Drawing Instruments, Lines, Geometric figures, Symbolic Representation), Units & Measurements, Mass Weight & Density, Work Power & Energy, Speed & Velocity, Heat & Temperature, Basic Electricity, Levers & Simple Machines, Occupational Safety & Health, Environment Education, IT Literacy.
       * **Mathematics (25 Qs):** Number System, BODMAS, Fractions, LCM-HCF, Ratio, Percentages, Mensuration, Time & Work, Time & Distance, CI/SI, Profit-Loss, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics, Square Root, Age, Calendar-Clock, Pipes.
       * **General Intelligence & Reasoning (25 Qs):** Syllogism, Analogies, Coding-Decoding, Venn Diagrams, Puzzles, Blood Relations, Directions, Data Sufficiency.
       * **General Awareness (10 Qs):** Current Affairs, Science & Tech, Sports, Polity.
   * **Part B (Qualifying Trade Test - 75 Questions / 60 Minutes / 75 Marks):**
     * **Syllabus:** Relevant Technical Trade syllabus prescribed by DGT (Directorate General of Training).
     * **🚨 CRITICAL PASS RULE:** **Mandatory 35% Qualifying Pass Mark (26.25 marks out of 75) for ALL candidates irrespective of UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS!** No relaxation whatsoever.
     * If candidate fails in Part-B, their Part-A score will **NOT be evaluated or considered** for CBAT shortlisting.
     * **Qualification-wise Trade Grouping for Diploma/Degree Candidates:**
       * Electrical Engg ➔ Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Wireman, Armature & Coil Winder, RAC Mechanic.
       * Electronics Engg ➔ Electronics Mechanic, Mechanic (Radio & TV).
       * Mechanical Engg ➔ Fitter, Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Tractor Mechanic, Mechanic Diesel, Turner, Machinist, RAC Mechanic, Heat Engine, Millwright/Maintenance Mechanic.
       * Automobile Engg ➔ Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Tractor Mechanic, Mechanic Diesel, Heat Engine, RAC Mechanic.

3. **Stage 3: CBAT (Computer Based Aptitude Test / Psycho):**
   * **Shortlisted Candidates:** **8 times** RRB-wise vacancies based on CBT-2 Part-A marks (only for candidates qualifying Part-B).
   * **Mandatory Entry Requirement:** Original Self-Screening Medical Declaration Report (Annexures VIII-A & VIII-B).
   * **Batteries:** 5 Test Batteries (Memory, Direction Ability, Depth Perception, Concentration, Perceptual Speed).
   * **Qualifying Criterion:** Minimum T-Score of **42 marks in each test battery separately** for all candidates (no reservation concessions).
   * **Language & Marking:** English and Hindi only. No negative marking.

4. **Stage 4: Document Verification (DV) & Final Merit Formula:**
   * **🚨 CRITICAL FORMULA CHANGE IN CEN 01/2026 (Para 12.4):**
     * **Final Combined Merit = 50% Weightage to CBT-2 Part A + 50% Weightage to CBAT (Psycho) Score!**
     * *(Historical 2018/2024 cycle was 70:30; CEN 01/2026 officially establishes equal 50:50 weightage).*
   * Shortlisted for DV in exact **1:1 ratio** matching vacancies.
   * Tie-breaker: Older candidate ranked higher; if same, alphabetical order of given name.

5. **Stage 5: Medical Examination (ME):**
   * Comprehensive A-1 fitness check by Railway Medical Board.

6. **Stage 6: Final Panel & ZRTI Training:**
   * Allotment to Railway Zones/Production Units and training at Zonal Railway Training Institutes.

#### 💰 Pay Scale & Age Limit:

* **Age Limit (as on 01.01.2027):** **20 to 33 Years** *(Note: Minimum age is 20 years, not 18!)*.
* **Date of Birth Limits (both dates inclusive):**
  * **UR & EWS:** 02.01.1994 to 01.01.2007
  * **OBC-Non Creamy Layer:** 02.01.1991 to 01.01.2007 (3 Years relaxation)
  * **SC / ST:** 02.01.1989 to 01.01.2007 (5 Years relaxation)
  * **Ex-Servicemen:** Service period + 3 yrs (UR/EWS), +6 yrs (OBC), +8 yrs (SC/ST).

#### 🎓 Prescribed Minimum Educational Qualification:
* **Graduate Degree** from a recognized University or its equivalent.
* *(Note: Station Master post is NOT suitable for PwBD candidates — VI/HI/LD/OD/MD = No suitability).*

#### 👁️ Strict A-2 Medical Standards (Para 6.0 & Annexure-VI):
* **Physical Fitness:** Physically fit in all respects.
* **Distant Vision:** **6/9, 6/9 without glasses** (No fogging test permissible, eye sight must be unaided!).
* **Near Vision:** **Sn: 0.6, 0.6 without glasses** for close instrument/signal monitoring.
* **Special Eye Tests:** Must pass tests for Colour Vision (Ishihara / lantern), Binocular Vision, Field of Vision, Night Vision, and Mesopic Vision.
* **Strict LASIK Prohibition:** Any candidate with a history of **LASIK / Refractive Surgery / Lens Implants** is **STRICTLY DISQUALIFIED** (Annexure VI-B & VI-C).
* **Mandatory Vision Certificate:** Shortlisted candidates must present the original Medical Certificate from Eye Specialist in prescribed format (**Annexure-VI / Annexure-VIA**) during CBAT, failing which entry will be denied.

#### 📝 Selection Stages & Exam Pattern:

1. **Stage 1: Single Computer Based Test (CBT) — [120 Minutes / 100 Questions / 100 Marks]**
   * **Total Duration:** 120 Minutes (2 Hours) [160 Mins for eligible PwBD scribes].
   * **Total Questions:** 100 Objective MCQs (1 mark each).
   * **Negative Marking:** 1/3rd mark deducted per incorrect answer.
   * **Normalization:** Multi-shift normalization applies.
   * **Screening Nature:** Normalized CBT marks shortlist candidates for CBAT at **8 times (8x)** the vacancies.
   * **Section-wise Question Breakdown:**
     * **Section 1: Basic Science, Mathematical and Analytical Capability — 60 Questions (60 Marks)**
       * *Part A: Basic Science & Mathematics (30 Marks):* Number System, Ratio & Proportions, Averages, Percentages, Profit, Loss & Discounts, Time, Speed & Distance, Power & Work, Algebra, Linear Equations, Arithmetic Progression (AP), LCM, HCF, Geometry and Trigonometry, Area & Volumes, Elementary Probability & Statistics. Basic Science: Units, Measurements, Mass Weight and Density, Work Power Energy, Kinetic Energy, Friction, Speed & Velocity, Relative Velocity, Heat & Temperature, Basic Electricity, Levers & Simple Machines.
       * *Part B: Data Analysis & Interpretation (30 Marks):* Multi-source Data Analysis — examination, analysis and inference drawing from text, tabular data and graphical data (bar charts, line graphs, scatter plots, pie charts, statistical curve distributions, Venn diagrams, Data Sufficiency, Data Arrangement).
     * **Section 2: Logical Capability — 20 Questions (20 Marks)**
       * *Logical Reasoning:* Binary Logic (truth-tellers and liars), Syllogism, Clocks & Calendars, Assumptions, Blood Relations, Family Tree, Logic-based puzzles.
       * *Reading Comprehension:* Passages in English from History, Society, Literature, Science, Environment, Abstract, Mythology, Technology & Culture (Main idea, Supporting idea, Application, Logical Structure & Style).
     * **Section 3: Mental Reasoning — 20 Questions (20 Marks)**
       * Analogy (relationship matching), Series Completion (pattern recognition), Coding-Decoding, Ranking & Order, Seating & Direction Arrangements.
   * **Qualifying Cut-off:** UR: 40%, EWS: 40%, OBC(NCL): 30%, SC: 30%, ST: 25%.

2. **Stage 2: Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT / Psycho Test):**
   * **Shortlisting Ratio:** **8 times (8x)** the number of vacancies (RRB-wise and category-wise).
   * **Test Batteries:** 5 standard RDSO Test Batteries:
     1. Intelligence / Classification Test
     2. Selective Attention Test (Odd Number Sum / Vigilance)
     3. Spatial Scanning Test (Shortest Route on track grid)
     4. Information Ordering Test (Signal/machine operation sequence)
     5. Personality / Attitude Test (Safety mindset & decision making)
   * **Qualifying Criteria:** Must secure a **minimum T-Score of 42 marks in EACH test battery separately**. No category relaxation is permissible!
   * **No Negative Marking** in CBAT.
   * **Language:** Test displayed in English and Hindi only.

3. **Stage 3: Combined Merit & Document Verification (DV):**
   * **Merit Formula:** **70% CBT Marks + 30% CBAT Marks**!
   * **Call Ratio:** Called in exact **1:1 ratio** of vacancies based on combined merit.
   * **Tie-Breaker:** Older candidate gets higher merit; if age is same, alphabetical order of given first name (A to Z).

4. **Stage 4: Medical Examination & Final Panel:**
   * Passing Railway Medical Board's strict **A-2 standard** is mandatory for final appointment.

[Stage 1: Single CBT] ──► [Stage 2: PET (Physical)] ──► [Stage 3: DV (1:1 Ratio)] ──► [Stage 4: Medical Exam (A2/A3/B1/C1)] ──► [Final Empanelment]

#### 📅 Important Dates (CEN 09/2025):
* **Date of Indicative Notice in Employment News:** 27.12.2025
* **Opening Date of Online Application:** 31.01.2026 (00:00 Hrs)
* **Closing Date of Online Application:** 02.03.2026 (23:59 Hrs)
* **Last Date for Application Fee Payment:** 04.03.2026 (23:59 Hrs)
* **Modification Window for Corrections:** 05.03.2026 to 14.03.2026 (23:59 Hrs)
* **Scribe Details Submission Window:** 15.03.2026 to 19.03.2026

#### 💰 Pay Scale & Age Limit:
* **Pay Level:** Level-1 in 7th CPC Pay Matrix (Initial Pay ₹18,000/- per month + DA, HRA, Transport Allowance).
* **Age Limit (as on 01.01.2026):** **18 – 33 Years**
  * UR & EWS: Born between 02.01.1993 and 01.01.2008
  * OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): Born between 02.01.1990 and 01.01.2008 (+3 Years relaxation)
  * SC / ST: Born between 02.01.1988 and 01.01.2008 (+5 Years relaxation)
  * PwBD: +10 Years (UR), +13 Years (OBC), +15 Years (SC/ST)
  * Ex-Servicemen: Length of service + 3 years (UR), +6 years (OBC), +8 years (SC/ST)

#### 🎓 Prescribed Minimum Educational Qualification (CRITICAL RULE):
* **"10th pass OR ITI OR equivalent OR National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT"**
* 💡 **Key Candidate Relief:** *Simple 10th pass candidates are completely eligible to apply without any ITI certificate for all notified posts!*
* Engineering Diploma / Degree is NOT accepted in lieu of ITI / CCAA for Level-1.

#### 📝 Selection Stages & Exam Pattern:
1. **Stage 1: Single-Stage CBT (Computer Based Test):**
   * **Pattern:** 100 Questions | 90 Minutes (120 Mins for eligible scribe candidates) | 100 Marks.
   * **Negative Marking:** 1/3rd mark deducted for each wrong answer (-0.33).
   * **Subject Breakdown:**
     * **General Intelligence & Reasoning:** 30 Questions (30 Marks)
     * **Mathematics:** 25 Questions (25 Marks)
     * **General Science:** 25 Questions (25 Marks - 10th CBSE NCERT Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
     * **General Awareness & Current Affairs:** 20 Questions (20 Marks - Science & Tech, Sports, Economics, Polity)
   * **Qualifying Pass %:** UR: 40%, EWS: 40%, OBC (NCL): 30%, SC: 30%, ST: 30% (Relaxable by 2% for PwBD in case of shortage).
   * **Shortlisting for PET:** **3 times (3x)** the community-wise vacancies based on normalized CBT marks.

2. **Stage 2: Physical Efficiency Test (PET - Mandatory & Qualifying):**
   * Test conducted in two sequential stages (Weight carry first, then running after recovery gap):
   * **Male Candidates:**
     1. Lift & carry **35 kg weight for 100 meters in 2 minutes** in one chance without putting the weight down.
     2. Run for **1,000 meters (1 km) in 4 minutes and 15 seconds** in one chance.
   * **Female & Transgender Candidates:**
     1. Lift & carry **20 kg weight for 100 meters in 2 minutes** in one chance without putting the weight down.
     2. Run for **1,000 meters (1 km) in 5 minutes and 40 seconds** in one chance.
   * **🚨 PET Exemptions:**
     * **PwBD candidates** are 100% exempted from PET (must pass PwBD medical standard after CBT).
     * **Course Completed Act Apprentices (CCAA)** are 100% exempted from PET.
     * **Ex-Servicemen (ExSM)** are 100% exempted from PET.
   * **⭐ Railway Act Apprentices (CCAA) 1/3rd Weightage (Para 12.0):**
     * CCAA candidates receive **one-third (1/3rd) weightage** of their NCVT marks directly added to their CBT score in preparation of the final merit list!

3. **Stage 3: Document Verification (DV):**
   * Called in exact **1:1 ratio** based on merit in CBT (subject to qualifying PET).
   * Tie-breaker: Older candidate ranked higher; then alphabetical order of given name.

4. **Stage 4: Medical Examination (ME):**
   * **A-2:** Pointsman B (Distant 6/9 without glasses, near Sn 0.6 without glasses, color vision).
   * **A-3:** Assistant Track Machine (Distant 6/9 with/without glasses up to 2D, color vision).
   * **B-1:** Track Maintainer Gr IV, Assistant Bridge, Assistant Carriage & Wagon, Assistant Loco Shed, Assistant P.Way, Assistant S&T (Signal), Assistant TRD, Assistant TL & AC (Distant 6/9, 6/12 with/without glasses up to 4D, color vision).
   * **C-1:** Assistant Operations Electrical (Workshop), Assistant S&T (Workshop) (Distant 6/12, 6/18 with/without glasses, reading glasses allowed).

5. **Stage 5: Final Empanelment & Division Allotment:**
   * Empanelled by respective RRCs across 16 Railway Zones (Western, North Western, Northern, Central, Eastern, East Central, Southern, South Central, South Eastern, etc.).
