## 🎯 Step-by-Step Complete Selection Pipeline (Exam-by-Exam)

---

### 1. 🚆 RRB ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot) — 18,799 Posts
```
[Stage 1: CBT-1] ──► [Stage 2: CBT-2 (Part A + B)] ──► [Stage 3: CBAT (Psycho)] ──► [Stage 4: DV] ──► [Stage 5: Medical (A-1)] ──► [Final Merit]
```
1. **Stage 1: CBT-1 (Screening Test):**
   * 75 Qs | 60 Mins | 75 Marks | Neg: -1/3.
   * Maths (20), Reasoning (25), General Science (20), GA (10).
   * Shortlisted for CBT-2: **15 times vacancy**.
2. **Stage 2: CBT-2 (Part A + Part B in 1 sitting - 2 Hr 30 Min):**
   * **Part A (100 Qs / 90 Mins) [Merit Decider]:**
     * Basic Science & Engineering (BSED): 40 Qs
     * Mathematics: 25 Qs | Reasoning: 25 Qs | Current Affairs: 10 Qs.
   * **Part B (75 Qs / 60 Mins) [Qualifying Trade Test]:**
     * Electrician, Fitter, Mechanical, Diesel, Electronics trade.
     * **Mandatory 35% marks (26.25/75)**. If fail in Part B, Part A is NOT checked!
   * Shortlisted for CBAT: **8 times vacancy**.
3. **Stage 3: CBAT (Psycho Aptitude Test):**
   * 5 Test Batteries (Memory, Direction, Depth, Concentration, Speed).
   * Minimum T-Score 42 in each battery (No negative marking).
   * **Merit Weightage:** 70% of CBT-2 Part A + 30% of CBAT.
4. **Stage 4: Document Verification (DV):**
   * 1:1 ratio based on merit. Original certificates + Caste/EWS/OBC verification.
5. **Stage 5: Medical Examination (Strict Medical Standard A-1):**
   * **Distance Vision:** 6/6, 6/6 without glasses (No glasses, No LASIK permitted!).
   * Near Vision: Sn: 0.6 without glasses. Full color vision (Ishihara test).
6. **Stage 6: Final Panel & Appointment Result.**

### 2. 📚 RRB NTPC (Graduate 8,113 Posts & UG 3,445 Posts)
```
[Stage 1: CBT-1] ──► [Stage 2: CBT-2] ──► [Stage 3: CBAT / Typing (TST)] ──► [Stage 4: DV] ──► [Stage 5: Medical (A2/A3/B2/C2)] ──► [Final Merit]
```
1. **Stage 1: CBT-1 (Common Screening - 100 Qs / 90 Mins):**
   * GA (40), Maths (30), Reasoning (30) = 100 Marks (Neg: -1/3).
   * Shortlisted for CBT-2: **15 times vacancy per level**.
2. **Stage 2: CBT-2 (Separate for Levels 2, 3, 5, 6 - 120 Qs / 90 Mins):**
   * GA (50), Maths (35), Reasoning (35) = 120 Marks.
3. **Stage 3: Skill Test (Post-wise):**
   * **CBAT (Psycho):** ONLY for Station Master (Level 6) & Traffic Assistant. (70% CBT-2 + 30% CBAT).
   * **Typing Skill Test (TST):** For Junior Clerk, Senior Clerk, Accounts Typist. (30 wpm English OR 25 wpm Hindi, Qualifying).
   * **Direct DV (No Skill Test):** For Goods Train Manager, Commercial Apprentice, Commercial cum Ticket Clerk.
4. **Stage 4: Document Verification (DV):**
   * 1:1 Ratio.
5. **Stage 5: Medical Examination:**
   * Station Master: Medical Standard **A-2** (6/9, 6/9 without glasses).
   * Goods Train Manager: Medical Standard **A-2**.
   * Clerical/Typist Posts: Medical Standard **C-2** (Glasses allowed).
6. **Stage 6: Final Selection & Zonal Allotment.**

### 3. 🔧 RRB Technician (CEN 02/2024 - 9,144 Posts) — *Major Pattern Change!*
```
[Stage 1: Single-Stage CBT] ──► [Stage 2: DV] ──► [Stage 3: Medical Exam (A3/B1)] ──► [Final Merit Result]
```
*(NOTE: In 2024, CBT-2 was completely removed! Only 1 CBT exam decides final merit!)*

1. **Stage 1: Single Stage CBT (100 Qs / 90 Mins):**
   * **Technician Grade 3 (ITI Holders):**
     * General Science (10th CBSE): **40 Qs** *(Huge Weightage!)*
     * Maths: 25 Qs | Reasoning: 25 Qs | GA: 10 Qs.
   * **Technician Grade 1 Signal (Diploma/Degree):**
     * Basic Science & Engg: 35 Qs | Computer Basics & IT: 20 Qs | Maths: 20 Qs | Reasoning: 15 Qs | GA: 10 Qs.
2. **Stage 2: Document Verification (DV):** 1:1 ratio.
3. **Stage 3: Medical Examination (B-1 / B-2 / A-3 as per trade).**
4. **Stage 4: Final Merit List (100% based on Single CBT Marks).**
