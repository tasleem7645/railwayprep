# 🚆 Official Indian Railway Exams Complete Syllabus & Pattern Blueprint (2026-27)
*Compiled from Official Centralised Employment Notices (CEN 01/2024, CEN 02/2024, CEN 05/2024, CEN 06/2024 & RRC Level 1)*

---

## 📌 Common Rules Across ALL Railway Exams:
* **Negative Marking:** $1/3^{\text{rd}}$ mark is deducted for every wrong answer.
* **Question Type:** Objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
* **Medium / Language:** Bilingual (Hindi, English + 13 Regional Languages).
* **Exam Engine:** TCS iON Computer-Based Test (CBT).

---

## 1. RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories)
*Target Posts: Station Master, Goods Train Manager, Senior Clerk, Junior Accounts Assistant, Commercial Apprentice, Typist*

### 🔹 Stage 1: CBT-1 (Screening Test)
* **Duration:** 90 Minutes (120 Mins for PwBD)
* **Total Questions:** 100 MCQs | **Total Marks:** 100

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Mathematics** | 30 | 30 |
| **General Intelligence & Reasoning** | 30 | 30 |
| **General Awareness (Science + GK + Current Affairs)** | 40 | 40 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

### 🔹 Stage 2: CBT-2 (Merit Stage - Selected 15x Candidates)
* **Duration:** 90 Minutes
* **Total Questions:** 120 MCQs | **Total Marks:** 120

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Mathematics** | 35 | 35 |
| **General Intelligence & Reasoning** | 35 | 35 |
| **General Awareness** | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

### 📖 Detailed Subject-Wise Topics:
* **Mathematics:** Number System, Decimals, Fractions, LCM & HCF, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Mensuration (2D & 3D), Time & Work, Time Speed Distance, Simple & Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics.
* **Reasoning:** Analogies, Number & Alphabetical Series, Coding-Decoding, Mathematical Operations, Syllogism, Blood Relations, Venn Diagrams, Jumbling, Data Sufficiency, Statement-Conclusion, Decision Making, Direction Sense.
* **General Awareness:** 10th CBSE Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Indian Freedom Struggle & History, Indian Polity & Constitution, Geography (India & World), Indian Economy, Scientific & Space Tech (ISRO/DRDO), Environment Issues, Basics of Computers, Indian Railways GK, Current Affairs.

## 2. RRB ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot)
*Target Posts: Assistant Loco Pilot (Electric & Diesel Locomotives)*

### 🔹 Stage 1: CBT-1 (Screening - 75 Questions / 60 Minutes)
* **Duration:** 60 Minutes | **Total Marks:** 75

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Mathematics** | 20 | 20 |
| **General Intelligence & Reasoning** | 25 | 25 |
| **General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)** | 20 | 20 |
| **General Awareness & Current Affairs** | 10 | 10 |
| **Total** | **75** | **75** |

### 🔹 Stage 2: CBT-2 (Dual Stage - 2 Hours 30 Minutes Total)
* **Part A (100 Qs / 90 Mins) [Merit Decider]:**
  * Mathematics: 25 Qs
  * General Intelligence & Reasoning: 25 Qs
  * **Basic Science & Engineering Drawing (BSED):** 40 Qs *(Crucial Section!)*
  * General Awareness: 10 Qs
* **Part B (75 Qs / 60 Mins) [Qualifying Trade Test]:**
  * Relevant ITI/Diploma Trade (Electrician, Fitter, Mechanical, Electronic Mechanic, Turner, Wireman).
  * **Mandatory Passing Score:** 35% (26.25 Marks out of 75).

### 🔹 Stage 3: CBAT (Computer-Based Aptitude Test / Psycho Test)
* Memory Test, Following Direction Test, Depth Perception, Concentration Test, Perceptual Speed Test.

## 💡 Summary: Most Common Overlapping Subjects Across ALL Exams:
1. **General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology):** Heavy weightage in Group D (25 Qs), Technician (40 Qs), ALP (20 Qs), NTPC (~15 Qs).
2. **Arithmetic & Maths:** Common syllabus across all 5 exams (Percentages, CI/SI, Ratio, Time & Work, Speed, Geometry).
3. **Reasoning:** Syllogism, Series, Coding-Decoding, Clock & Calendar are universally tested.
